## Mekanism GUI textures

The machine screen textures listed below were copied from Mekanism
(<https://github.com/mekanism/Mekanism>, version 10.7.19 for Minecraft 1.21.1) and renamed. Some files were
split into several sprites (button states, switch halves) and received
`.mcmeta` GUI scaling metadata; the pixels were not otherwise modified.

All files are under `src/main/resources/assets/dimensionalfluxminer/textures/gui/sprites/`.
